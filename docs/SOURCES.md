## DGFT website terms reviewed

DGFT's [Website Policies](https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/website-policy) copyright policy says
website material may be reproduced free of charge **after taking proper permission**; it
must be reproduced accurately, not used in a derogatory or misleading context, and the
source must be prominently acknowledged. It excludes third-party-copyright material.
Therefore, do not treat "public DGFT document" as blanket permission to republish full
documents or extracted tables. Obtain permission where required and check any document-
specific rights before redistribution. Linking to DGFT is permitted by its hyperlinking
policy.

DGFT's [Terms & Conditions](https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/terms-conditions) also says its
website content should not be construed as law or used for legal purposes, and advises
users to verify ambiguities and obtain appropriate professional advice. This inventory
is a source log, not legal advice. For answers, cite the controlling notification/public
notice number and date and verify the current instrument.

DGFT's [Disclaimer](https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=Disclaimer) states: "Content
published on this website have no legal sanctity and are for general reference only."
It directs visitors to official DGFT documents to ascertain facts. Prefer the actual
notification, public notice, FTP/HBP chapter, or schedule attachment over a portal
summary.
