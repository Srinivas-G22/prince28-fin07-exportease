# Real user questions: ExportEase

Written the way my user types them. User: Owner of a knitwear export unit in Tiruppur shipping to the EU who wants to claim RoDTEP and use Advance Authorisation.

These 10 questions become my first evaluation set in Week 3. Question 6 is not answerable from my documents: the product must say "I don't know".

| # | Question | Type | Where the answer is (document, page) | More than one page? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is RoDTEP and how is it claimed? | simple | FTP 2023, Chapter 4 (Para 4.54–4.59) & HBP 2023, Chapter 4 (Para 4.94–4.96) | No |
| 2 | What is an Advance Authorisation? | simple | FTP 2023, Chapter 4 (Para 4.03–4.05) | No |
| 3 | What is the RoDTEP rate for HS code 6109 (cotton T-shirts)? | simple | Appendix 4R (RoDTEP Schedule) — HS 6109 / 61091000 | No |
| 4 | We export cotton knitted T-shirts from Tiruppur to Germany worth Rs 50 lakh a month and import special yarn. Which schemes can we use, what documents do we need, and what is the estimated RoDTEP benefit? | multi-step (several pages) | FTP 2023 Ch. 4 (Advance Authorisation & RoDTEP), HBP 2023 Ch. 4 (AA documents & SION), Appendix 4R (HS 6109 rate) | Yes |
| 5 | Compare Advance Authorisation and the EPCG scheme. | comparison | FTP 2023, Chapter 4 (Advance Authorisation, Para 4.03) & Chapter 5 (EPCG, Para 5.01–5.04) | Yes |
| 6 | When will my pending RoDTEP credit be released? | unanswerable: must reply "I don't know" | none: not in any document | - |
| 7 | If we import dyed synthetic yarn under Advance Authorisation for our EU knitwear order, can we still claim RoDTEP on the final exported garments? | amendment / eligibility rule | FTP 2023, Para 4.55 (Ineligible categories) + DGFT Notification restoring/amending RoDTEP for AA/EOU/SEZ holders (e.g., Notification No. 70/2023 or latest amending notification) | Yes |
| 8 | Did DGFT change the RoDTEP rate or value cap per piece for HS 61091000 recently, and what is the active rate right now? | amendment / table lookup | Appendix 4R (RoDTEP Schedule) + DGFT Notification revising Appendix 4R rates/caps (e.g., Notification No. 32/2024-25 or latest schedule revision) | Yes |
| 9 | We are shipping a mixed container to France with cotton knitted hoodies (HS 61102000) and cotton baby garments (HS 61112000). Give me the RoDTEP rate, per-piece cap, and export policy for both. | multi-HS lookup | Appendix 4R (RoDTEP Schedule) & ITC(HS) Export Policy Schedule 2 — Chapter 61 (HS 6110 & 6111) | Yes |
| 10 | What is the minimum value addition required for Advance Authorisation in garments, and how many months do we get to complete the export obligation? | multi-clause rule | FTP 2023, Chapter 4 (Para 4.09 Minimum Value Addition & Para 4.22 Export Obligation Period) + HBP 2023, Para 4.40 | Yes |
