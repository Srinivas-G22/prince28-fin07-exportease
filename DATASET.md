# Dataset: ExportEase

Answers export policy questions for small exporters: Foreign Trade Policy rules, RoDTEP rates, Advance Authorisation and documentation, citing DGFT notifications.

- **Start target:** FTP 2023 + Handbook + 200 notices
- **Full target:** + all amendments, RoDTEP schedules and ITC(HS) policy (6,000+ pages)
- **Collected by:** `scripts/fetch.py` as `examate-student-bot` (https://github.com/Srinivas-G22/prince28-fin07-exportease): robots.txt respected, at most 4 requests at once, 0.5 s between requests to one site.

## Where the documents come from

- https://www.dgft.gov.in/

## Terms I follow

Public DGFT documents. Cite notification/public notice number and date.

- Every answer links to the original document.
- No personal data is collected.
